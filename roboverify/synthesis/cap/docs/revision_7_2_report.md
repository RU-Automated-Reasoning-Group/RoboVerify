# Revision 7.2 report: RDP recovery keyframes

- **Date:** 2026-10-26. Design: [revision_7_2_recovery_key_frame.md](revision_7_2_recovery_key_frame.md). Layout change: [Revision 7.3, §2](revision_7_3_frozen_v7.md#2-recovery-keyframe-layout-recovery-keyframes-v2).
- **Question:** does showing the failed rollout in the demonstration keyframe format help recovery more than the legacy three keyframes, and does the layout of that section matter?
- **Answer:** on the 49 trials whose round 0 failed:

| Arm | Recovered | Discordant pairs | McNemar (exact, two-sided) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| legacy | 33/49 | – | – |
| RDP v1 | 40/49 | vs legacy: 11 only RDP v1, 4 only legacy | p = 0.12 |
| RDP v2 | 42/49 | vs legacy: 11 only RDP v2, 2 only legacy | p = 0.022 |

  - RDP v2 recovers more seeds than legacy.
  - The two RDP arms do not differ measurably: 5 seeds recovered only by v2 and 3 only by v1 (p = 0.73). The layout change (Revision 7.3) neither helped nor hurt detectably. v1 and v2 are best read as two samples of the same RDP content.

## 3. Observations

- Reverse benefits most. In the legacy arm, 8 of its 10 Reverse failures executed all five rounds to the end without reaching the goal, so the error lies in the sequence of transfers, which the legacy frames (start, last primitive, end) do not show; the RDP frames list every pick and release with the ON/ON_star changes. Both RDP arms also need fewer rounds there (2.20 and 2.35 vs 2.85).
- ReStack gains little (13 and 14 vs 12 of 18). Its failures are mostly primitive nonconvergence or failed selections, where the failure location and primitive diagnostics, shared by all arms, carry the relevant information.
- Stack, Unstack and Grid recover in every arm; the differences are in rounds and tokens only.
- Repeating the RDP arm (v1, v2) changed 8 of 49 outcomes, which indicates the sampling variation of one run of rounds 1–4. The legacy arm was sampled once; ten seeds failing with legacy keyframes but recovering in both RDP runs is the main evidence for the method effect.
- Decision (2026-10-26): `config_frozen_v7` uses RDP keyframes with the v2 layout ([Revision 7.3](revision_7_3_frozen_v7.md)).
